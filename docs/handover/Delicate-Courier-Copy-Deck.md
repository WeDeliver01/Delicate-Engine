## FAQ

Heading: Frequently Asked Questions
Intro: Please reach us at support@delicatecourier.co.za if you cannot find an answer to your question.

How much is a standard delivery?
Our delivery rates are based on the exact collection and delivery addresses. To get a quick quote, you can use our Quick Quote tool. It's fast and accurate. Let us know if you need help with it.

Do you deliver all over Gauteng?
We deliver in most parts of Gauteng. If your area doesn't show up on our Quick Quote tool, please send us an email at support@delicatecourier.co.za and we'll gladly assist you.

Is it same-day delivery?
Yes, it is. We offer same-day delivery as standard for all bookings. Just make sure to book early enough in the day.

What are your delivery hours?
We deliver from Monday to Saturday, between 08:00 and 16:00. Let us know if you have special timing requests.

Do you only deliver cakes?
Not at all. We deliver a range of perishable goods, including cakes, baked treats, fresh flowers, and food platters. If it needs a little extra care, we're the team to help.

What happens after I book?
Once your booking is confirmed, our system automatically assigns a driver. You'll receive live updates via SMS or email so you're always in the loop.
